INSTALL THE BETA
1. After this welcome message, watch for a separate email from Firebase App Distribution. That second email contains your private installation invitation.
2. Open it using {{TESTER_EMAIL}}, the same Google email used for your beta invitation.
3. Accept the invitation, download AudioChoice, and allow Android to install the beta when prompted.
4. Future beta updates will arrive through Firebase using the same email.

The Firebase invitation may arrive a few minutes later. Check Spam or Promotions if you do not see it.

WHAT TO TEST
Please test importing, library behavior, playback, chapters, bookmarks, sleep timer, saved playback position, and general stability. For filters, tell us if a filter starts or ends at the wrong time, misses content, or skips clean content.

AUDIBLE
On a computer, sign in to Audible, open Library, and choose Download beside a supported title you legitimately own. Transfer the file to Android using USB, Google Drive, or another private method. In AudioChoice, open Import and select the file. AAX conversion happens locally on your device.

LIBRO.FM
Sign in at https://libro.fm/user/library, select Files beside your book, and choose M4B when available. Transfer it to your phone if needed, then select it from AudioChoice's Import screen.

FEEDBACK
Use Profile > Submit Feedback in the app, or join Discord: https://discord.gg/Nr5p6Vhes
Please include the book, chapter, approximate timestamp, and what should have happened.
