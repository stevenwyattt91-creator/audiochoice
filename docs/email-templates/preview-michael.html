Install the beta

  1. After this welcome message, watch for a separate email from Firebase App Distribution. That second email contains your private installation invitation.

  3. Accept the invitation, download AudioChoice, and allow Android to install the beta when prompted.
  4. Future beta updates will arrive through Firebase using the same email.
Important: The Firebase invitation is sent separately and may arrive a few minutes after this email. Check your Spam or Promotions folder if you do not see it.
What we need you to test Importing, library behavior, playback, chapters, bookmarks, sleep timer, playback position, and overall stability—plus whether filters start and end at the right moments, miss content, or skip clean content by mistake.

Bring your audiobook into AudioChoice

Audible

  1. On a computer, sign in at Audible and open Library.
  2. Find a supported title and choose Download. Only titles you legitimately own are eligible.
  3. Transfer the downloaded file to your Android phone with USB, Google Drive, or another private file-transfer method.
  4. In AudioChoice, open Import and select that file. AAX conversion happens privately on your device.

Libro.fm

  1. Sign in at libro.fm/user/library and select Files beside your book.
  2. Choose M4B when available; it provides the entire audiobook as one DRM-free file.
  3. Transfer the M4B to your phone if downloaded on a computer, then select it from AudioChoice’s Import screen.

Send feedback

In the app, open Profile → Submit Feedback. For discussion and quick questions, join the AudioChoice Discord community. Include the book, chapter, approximate timestamp, and what should have happened whenever possible.
