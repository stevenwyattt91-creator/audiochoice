Google sign-in, playback after a pause, and cover art.

Google sign-in could fail with an opaque error after the app had been rotated,
switched between light and dark, or left long enough for Android to reclaim the
screen. Sign-in was holding on to the screen it was first built with, which by
then no longer existed. It now uses whatever screen is actually in front.

Note: the other half of the sign-in problem was a certificate fingerprint that
was never registered with Google for this beta app. That was fixed on the
server side and needs no app update.

Pausing, locking the phone, then coming back left the player unable to play,
seek or do anything, with no error shown. A paused playback service is not a
foreground service, so Android is free to stop it while you are away, and the
app kept talking to the session that went with it. The app now notices the
session is gone, rebuilds it when you return, and reloads the book at the exact
position you paused it. Still paused, so nothing starts on its own.

Cover art is back. The artwork was never lost, it was on the phone the whole
time. The library loads from the phone first and the covers were being left out
of that first load, so they only reappeared once the app had finished checking
in with the server, and stayed blank for good if that check did not complete.
Covers now appear the moment the library opens.

Also in this build: the playback speed control is a - / + stepper that moves in
0.05 steps between 0.25x and 2.0x, and a book's own filters and bookmarks show
immediately on open instead of waiting on the network.
