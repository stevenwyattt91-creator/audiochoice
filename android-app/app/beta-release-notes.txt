The filter scanner now looks only for sexual content.

Profanity, violence, drug/alcohol references, blasphemy, and self-harm are no
longer scanned for or filterable -- those switches are gone from the filter
screen. The sexual-content filters themselves got more sensitive: innuendo,
euphemism, and crude-but-non-sexual banter are now judged more carefully, with
a lower bar for anything that sexualizes a woman specifically. Every book will
get a fresh scan the next time you open it to pick up the new rules.

The backend this app talks to has moved. You shouldn't notice anything
different, but if you see a sign-in or library error after updating, let us
know.

Earlier in this run of builds: the library opens instantly from your phone's
own copy instead of showing a loading bar, cover art and Explore load from
cache immediately, pausing/locking the phone and coming back no longer leaves
the player stuck, Google sign-in no longer breaks after the app is rotated or
backgrounded, and playback speed is a -/+ stepper moving in 0.05 steps between
0.25x and 2.0x.

Fixed: the private upload progress percentage wasn't moving during an
upload (it would jump straight from 0% to 100%). It now climbs steadily
while the file uploads, same as before.
