Tell us when a filter gets it wrong
- Tap the flag in the player the moment you hear something that should have been filtered. One tap, no form, and it works with no signal
- A report records where you were, never what you heard. No audio and no text leaves your phone
- In a book's Playback Filters, each control now has a flag to report that it removed something it should not have. That is the one that fixes over-filtering, because it tells us exactly which control fired
- Turning a filter off only helps you; reporting it helps everyone listening to the same recording
Finished books
- Books are marked finished when you reach the end, and covers show a green tick
- You can mark a book finished yourself from its details, for when you stop before the credits
- This never worked before: finishing a book left no trace
Explore is tidier
- The same audiobook no longer appears several times because two people's files disagreed about the author or carried a different edition note
- Different editions and separate parts are still listed separately, because those are genuinely different recordings with their own filter data
Please report anything odd in Discord.
