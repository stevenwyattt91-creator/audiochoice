Cover art is back.

The library had started opening with every cover blank. The artwork was never
lost -- it was on the phone the whole time. The library now loads from the
phone first, and the covers were being left out of that first load, so they
only reappeared once the app had finished checking in with the server, and
stayed blank for good if that check didn't complete.

Covers now appear the moment the library opens, from the phone, with no wait.

Also in this build: the playback speed control is a - / + stepper that moves in
0.05 steps between 0.25x and 2.0x, and a book's own filters and bookmarks show
immediately on open instead of waiting on the network.
