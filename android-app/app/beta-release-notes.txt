Two fixes for things that needed the app closed and reopened.

Pausing, locking the phone, then coming back left the player unable to play,
seek or do anything, with no error shown. A paused playback service is not a
foreground service, so Android is free to stop it while you are away. The app
kept talking to the session that went with it, so every button press went
nowhere. The app now notices that the session is gone, rebuilds it when you
return, and reloads the book at the exact position you paused it. Still paused,
so nothing starts playing on its own.

Cover art is back. The library had started opening with every cover blank. The
artwork was never lost, it was on the phone the whole time. The library loads
from the phone first and the covers were being left out of that first load, so
they only reappeared once the app had finished checking in with the server, and
stayed blank for good if that check did not complete. Covers now appear the
moment the library opens, with no wait.

Also in this build: the playback speed control is a - / + stepper that moves in
0.05 steps between 0.25x and 2.0x, and a book's own filters and bookmarks show
immediately on open instead of waiting on the network.
