The library opens instantly now.

Opening the app showed a loading bar and an empty screen for about ten seconds
before any books appeared. The books were already on the phone the whole time:
the app saves a copy of your library locally and loads that before it calls the
server, but the library screen was throwing it away and drawing only a progress
bar until the server answered. It now draws your books immediately and shows a
thin line under the title while it checks for updates in the background.

Explore is cached the same way, so the scanned catalogue is there the moment you
switch to it instead of waiting on a request.

Cover art appears straight away too, from the phone.

Earlier in this run of builds: pausing, locking the phone and coming back no
longer leaves the player unable to play, Google sign-in no longer breaks after
the app has been rotated or left in the background, and the playback speed
control is a - / + stepper moving in 0.05 steps between 0.25x and 2.0x.
