Playback now keeps going in the background
- Your audiobook no longer stops when you leave the app or lock your phone
- Play, pause and skip from the lock screen and the notification shade
- Playback pauses for phone calls, quiets for navigation, and pauses when you unplug headphones
- Your place is kept when you pause and close the app, even after swiping it away
- One Play button, and it always resumes where you stopped. "Start from Beginning" moved into the book's menu
Filters are more trustworthy
- If a book's filter data cannot load, the app now tells you instead of silently playing unfiltered audio
- Filter choices are no longer lost when a save fails; they sync in the background once you reconnect
Reading edition (attach an EPUB)
- Open a book, tap the book icon at the top of the player, and read along full screen
- Continuous scrolling, adjustable text size, light/sepia/dark themes and margins
- The text follows the narration and highlights the current passage; tap any paragraph to jump the audio there
- Filtered passages are removed from the text, not blacked out
- Your reading place is remembered per book
- If read-along timing is unavailable, the app now says why instead of giving one vague message
Titles and details
- Titles now come from the audiobook file's own details rather than its filename
- Narrator is shown when the file provides it
- Correct a title, author or narrator yourself from Edit Details in a book's menu
- After importing, the app says so when it only had a filename to go on
- A converted or re-tagged copy of an audiobook is now recognised as the same edition
Getting around
- Tapping a book in your library opens its details
- The green play button on Continue Listening goes straight to the player and resumes
Fixes
- Deleting a book now actually frees its storage, and imports no longer leave duplicate copies behind
- Leaving the app mid-import no longer drains the battery
- Flaky connections no longer create duplicate bookmarks
- An expired sign-in now asks you to sign in again instead of showing a password error
- Faster book opening, no freeze when entering a parental PIN, and fewer crashes on large cover art
- Larger tap targets, readable label sizes, and screen-reader support in the player and reader
Please report anything odd in Discord.
